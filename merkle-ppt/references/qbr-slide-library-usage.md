# QBR and business review slide library

Use the [156-page QBR collection](../assets/QBR%20SLIDE%20LIBRARY.pptx) and its [page-by-page index](qbr-slide-library-index.json) when choosing layouts for QBRs, recurring business reviews, project or program updates, executive reporting, stage reviews, retrospectives, planning, and decision-making. Its methods are not restricted to a quarter: adapt the time period and narrative to the actual assignment. Treat it as a curated reporting collection alongside the general [Slide Library](slide-library-usage.md), even when similar compositions appear in both; select by information relationship, available evidence and audience rather than forcing a particular source.

| Agenda and objectives | 14–22 | Workshops, leadership meetings and project kickoffs |

Each index entry records source page, design structure, prospective uses and adaptation guidance. Separators, source-only instructions, examples and closely related variants are indexed separately, not treated as fully populated content. Inspect the original page and its green, gray or yellow guidance notes before use. Some page counts for native charts and tables only describe explicit slide-level objects and may not reflect inherited content; inspect the original file before promising editability.

For actual data, distinguish facts, goals, hypotheses and recommendations; establish metric definitions, date ranges, denominators, baselines and sources, and only compare compatible measures. Populate source tables, charts, dates, screenshots, logos and sample content with project-approved material. Use native editable and groupable objects, retain automatic wrapping, preserve master/layout relationships, and check overflow and font substitution. When exact style matters, use the [QBR object-level source record](qbr-object-styles.json.gz) and [style guide](source-object-styles.md); source theme, effects, picture crops, gradients and inherited formatting may matter. Follow the user's current Arial / 微软雅黑, master, footer and closing rules over conflicting source instructions. Use source icons or verified visually identical artwork per [icon guidance](icon-usage.md).
