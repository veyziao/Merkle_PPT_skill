# Case study layout library

For detailed object styling, including fills, transparency, gradients, shadows and 3D, consult the [source object style records](source-object-styles.md) and the original source slide.

Use the [case study source deck](../assets/case%20library%20-%20Case%20Study%20Layout%20Types.pptx) and its [45-page index](case-library-index.json) alongside the general [slide library](slide-library-usage.md) when a page presents a client case, portfolio of cases, case evidence, or a project story. The index distinguishes instructions, section dividers, placeholders, examples, and the hidden style reference. The source slide number is preserved in each `CASE-###` ID.

## Choose by narrative and available material

| Need | Candidate source pages | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One case in a short executive summary | 8, 10–11, 13 | Use a dark image-led or light screenshot-led composition; include substantiated outcomes when available. |
| Several concise cases | 9, 12 | Compare brief case summaries in a four-up arrangement; reduce the number or split if content becomes dense. |
| One-page challenge, solution, and outcome | 15–18 | Use a detailed image-led page; page 18 shows an anonymous version. |
| One-page digital case with screenshots | 20–22 | Show the case narrative with a small number of relevant screenshots. |
| Additional screenshots | 24–26 | Show separate solutions or sequential aspects of one solution, with explanatory captions. |
| Additional services, deliverables, and impact | 28–29 | Expand the story across a second page, separating documented deliverables from evidenced outcomes. |
| Multi-page flagship story | 31–44 | Select relevant components: context, challenge, solutions, supporting visuals, and takeaways. |

Compare candidate layouts against the message, evidence, amount of content and available visuals; adapt them rather than copying the source story. Pages 1–7, 14, 19, 23, 27 and 30 are guide or section pages; page 3 is hidden. Pages labeled EXAMPLE or BLINDED EXAMPLE demonstrate a filled layout, not reusable project evidence. Page 45 is a closing reference; follow the current user's approved closing treatment.

The source recommends no more than 2–3 screenshots on the main short-form page and an additional page for further screenshots; the main case page should stand alone. A five-screen continuation can be adapted to other counts and aspect ratios. For anonymity, page 18 suggests replacing the client logo with an industry identifier while retaining the title and content position. Follow actual consent, confidentiality and project-specific instructions over the source's suggestions about identification.

Distinguish source guidance from the user's active requirements: use the approved Arial / 微软雅黑 typography, official template and master, native editable and groupable elements, and natural text wrapping. Sample logos, clients, metrics, screenshots, images, case narratives and outcomes are reference content; use them in a new project only when the user supplies or explicitly authorizes the relevant materials and facts.
